## VEGA

VEGA is a private, single-user productivity tool made by ZENVENT. It is not available to the public, and accounts can't be created.

It helps its owner manage their own email, calendar, and documents: summarizing messages, finding information, and keeping track of appointments. It uses Google sign-in only for the owner's account.

How data is handled is covered in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
